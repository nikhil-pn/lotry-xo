01 · How a round works

A coin with no key.

On Bitcoin Cash, money sits in coins, and every coin carries a small script that says how it may be spent. Most scripts ask for a signature from one key. The pot's script asks for none. It only lists the next moves it allows: one more ticket sold, the right winner written down, the winner paid, or a ticket refunded after the deadline.

No key means nothing to lose, leak, or run away with. The organiser runs the round but can never move the money.

  1. 1

    Open

    Organiser

    Creates the pot: a fresh coin carrying the round's minting NFT. It also publishes the hash of a secret it will reveal at the draw.

    pot 0 tickets

  2. 2

    Buy a ticket

    Organiser and player, together

    One transaction, two people. The organiser adds the pot, the player adds the price and receives ticket #n, a numbered NFT nobody can change.

    pot n tickets, + price

  3. 3

    Draw

    Organiser, after the draw height

    The organiser reveals the secret. The covenant checks it against the hash and works out the winner itself: sha256(secret) mod tickets + 1.

    pot winner #k

  4. 4

    Claim

    Whoever holds the winning ticket

    The winner spends the ticket together with the pot. Both NFTs are burned and the whole pot is paid out.

    pot paid to the winner

  5. 5

    or, if never drawn

    Refund

    Any ticket holder, after the refund height

    Each ticket burns for its price back. Nobody has to say yes, the organiser included.

    pot − price, ticket burned

The pot's state is its NFT commitment: a 4-byte ticket count while the round is open, then the winning number plus the byte 0xd0 once drawn. The covenant checks every move against it.
